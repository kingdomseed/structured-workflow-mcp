# Develop Feasible Ideas

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to B2 before starting. Read the Design Brief, B1 design specifications, prior-art analysis, current developing-ideas document, `GLOSSARY.md`, and relevant project and external sources.

## Create real alternatives

Develop at least two meaningfully different feasible ideas. Develop three when the search space supports a useful third direction. Vary the solution's structure, interface, interaction model, boundaries, or product framing rather than offering cosmetic versions of one idea.

For each idea, explain:

- its central concept and intended experience;
- how it works at the level needed to judge it;
- which design specifications it meets well or poorly;
- its evidence and relationship to prior art;
- its important trade-offs, risks, assumptions, and unknowns;
- what would make it infeasible;
- what evidence would increase confidence.

Lead the exploration with grounded recommendations while keeping the alternatives honest. Prefer project conventions and proven patterns when they satisfy the need. Include a less familiar direction when it tests a real assumption or could produce a better outcome.

Do not collapse the ideas into an unexamined mixture. If parts of several ideas appear compatible, form a new coherent idea, explain its internal logic, and evaluate it as its own candidate.

## Prototype to answer

Use a prototype when a consequential question cannot be answered well from existing evidence. State the question, expected evidence, success signal, time boundary, and disposal plan before creating it.

Shape the prototype around the question. A state or logic question needs a different artifact from a visual or interaction question. Keep the prototype separate from production work. Preserve the answer, feedback, screenshots, measurements, or decision; then delete the prototype or deliberately absorb validated parts through the normal creation process.

For visual work, treat a selected visual direction or existing source as the target. A written brief alone does not show the intended visual form. Present meaningfully different visual options and let the human select or revise one before creation.

When B2 was entered from A2 to answer an inquiry question, record the result as research evidence and project-created prior art, update the tracker, and reassess the next direction with the human.

## Record the output

Write the **B2 — Develop feasible ideas** section of the developing-ideas document. Include the ideas, evidence, prototype results, and unresolved trade-offs.

B2 is complete when the human can understand and compare a credible set of genuinely different ideas against B1.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` with the result, remaining questions, and recommended next move.
