# Develop Design Specifications

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to B1 before starting. Read the Design Brief, inquiry evidence, current developing-ideas document, `GLOSSARY.md`, project requirements, and relevant sources.

## Establish the measuring stick

Develop design specifications that state what a successful solution must achieve. Derive them from the justified need, affected people or systems, research, constraints, prior art, and project standards.

For each specification, record:

- a stable identifier;
- the required outcome or constraint;
- why it belongs and the evidence behind it;
- how an idea and the finished solution can be evaluated against it;
- its priority when the human has established one.

Write observable outcomes in plain language. Keep implementation choices out until the outcome itself requires a specific interface, platform, standard, or compatibility promise. Preserve hard constraints exactly.

Cover only the areas relevant to the work. These may include user behavior, functional outcomes, accessibility, performance, reliability, security, compatibility, data handling, visual or interaction quality, maintainability, and project Definition of Done.

Make every specification capable of supporting a real judgment. Use a command, measurement, inspection method, scenario, or numbered manual check when one is already knowable. Mark evidence that still needs to be designed in D1 rather than inventing a weak test.

Check the set as a whole for contradictions, accidental scope growth, missing states, and criteria that merely restate the proposed solution. Confirm that it describes the intended finished form. Treat staged scope as a human decision, not an automatic fallback.

## Record the output

Write the **B1 — Develop design specifications** section of the developing-ideas document. Trace each specification to the Design Brief or supporting evidence.

B1 is complete when the human agrees that the specifications form a clear and sufficient basis for developing and comparing ideas.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` with the result, remaining questions, and recommended next move.
