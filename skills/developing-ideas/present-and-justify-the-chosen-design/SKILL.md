# Present and Justify the Chosen Design

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to B3 before starting. Read the Design Brief, B1 design specifications, B2 feasible ideas and prototype evidence, current developing-ideas document, `GLOSSARY.md`, and relevant sources.

## Compare before choosing

Compare every credible idea against the same design specifications. Keep separate criteria visible so strength in one area cannot hide failure in another.

For each idea, explain:

- which specifications it satisfies and the supporting evidence;
- where it creates trade-offs or residual risk;
- which assumptions remain untested;
- how it affects the people, systems, constraints, and existing project;
- the confidence warranted by the evidence.

Use a table or scoring method only when it makes the reasoning easier to inspect. Avoid invented precision. Explain any weighting and let the human change it.

Lead with a recommendation. State why it best fits the whole set of criteria, where another idea is stronger, and what evidence could change the recommendation. The human chooses or approves the design.

If the preferred direction combines earlier ideas, present the combination as a coherent new design and compare it against the specifications. Resolve incompatible assumptions instead of hiding them inside a compromise.

## Record the chosen design

Describe the chosen design's shape, important behaviors, boundaries, interfaces, user experience, and relationship to the existing project at the level supported by current evidence.

Record:

- the chosen design;
- the human's decision;
- the full justification against B1;
- the important reasons other ideas were not chosen;
- accepted trade-offs and residual risks;
- prototype or visual targets that define the direction;
- decisions that B4 must make precise.

Create a decision record only when the choice is difficult to reverse, surprising without context, and based on a real trade-off.

## Record the output

Write the **B3 — Present and justify the chosen design** section of the developing-ideas document.

B3 is complete when one coherent design has human approval and its selection is traceable to the design specifications and evidence.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` with the result, remaining questions, and recommended next move.
