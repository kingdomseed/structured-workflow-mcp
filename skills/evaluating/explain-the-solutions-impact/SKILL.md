# Explain the Solution's Impact

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to D4 before starting. Read the A1 justified need, Design Brief, design specifications, chosen design, justified changes, D1 methods, D2 verdict, D3 dispositions, and direct evidence from the people or systems affected.

## Compare intended and observed change

Separate three levels:

- **output** — what was created or changed;
- **outcome** — what people or systems can now do or experience;
- **impact** — how that outcome affects the justified need over the relevant time and scope.

Explain:

- who or what was affected;
- the situation before the solution;
- the observed situation after the solution;
- evidence of benefit, harm, displacement, or no meaningful change;
- which intended outcomes occurred and which did not;
- unexpected effects and affected groups;
- limits in the evidence, time horizon, or attribution;
- what the result teaches the human and project.

Use direct feedback, observed behavior, support evidence, product or system measurements, comparison data, and other credible sources available for this solution. Keep claims proportional to the evidence. A working implementation proves output; it does not by itself prove outcome or impact.

When impact cannot yet be measured, state what is known, what remains too early or unavailable to judge, and which future method could produce the missing evidence.

## Close or reopen the cycle

Use D4 to explain whether the solution addressed the original justified need. Let unmet needs, new harms, changed circumstances, or newly visible opportunities seed A1 for another cycle. Carry forward the evidence rather than rewriting the history of the completed cycle.

Write the **D4 — Explain the solution's impact** section of the evaluation entry. Make it the terminal summary of the completed cycle when the evidence supports one.

D4 is complete when the human can understand what changed, for whom, how confidently it is known, and whether the next useful move is to close the work, keep observing, improve the solution, or begin another inquiry.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` with the cycle outcome and next direction.
