# Explain and Justify the Need

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to A1 before starting. Read the current inquiry document, settled glossary terms, the request, and project evidence that bears on the need.

Prefer retrieval-led reasoning over training-led reasoning. Research project facts and cite their sources. Ask the human for intent, experience, priorities, and judgment.

## Develop the justified need

Establish:

- the people or systems involved;
- their current situation;
- what the human wants to create, change, improve, prevent, preserve, or understand;
- why it is worth doing;
- why now, when timing matters;
- the boundaries of the inquiry;
- what a good outcome would make possible;
- what must be preserved.

Match the framing to the request. Describe a problem plainly when something is wrong or missing. Describe an opportunity in terms of what the human wants to create and why.

Explore credible accounts of the need before settling one. Show how each account changes the inquiry. Test the leading account against concrete scenarios, project evidence, and the human's experience.

Use the shared interview rounds established by `$start-inquire-and-analyze`. Develop questions from the evidence and explain why they matter. Use open questions for the human's aims and values.

## Record the output

Write the **A1 — Explain and justify the need** section of the inquiry document. Make the justification understandable to a smart reader who has not seen the conversation.

Separate supported facts, human judgments, inferences, and unknowns when the distinction matters. Cite material claims.

A1 is complete when the human confirms a clear, bounded, and justified account of what they want and why.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` with the result, remaining questions, and recommended next move.
