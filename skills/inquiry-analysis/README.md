# Inquire and Analyze

Inquire and Analyze builds a trustworthy understanding of what the human wants
to create, change, improve, prevent, preserve, or understand. It develops
questions from the request, project evidence, external sources, constraints, and
gaps in shared understanding. The phase produces an approved Design Brief.

The phase entry point is
[`$start-inquire-and-analyze`](start-inquire-and-analyze/SKILL.md).

## Criterion skills

1. [**A1 — Explain and justify the need**](explain-and-justify-the-need/SKILL.md)
   establishes what is wanted, who or what is affected, why it matters, and how
   the work is bounded.
2. [**A2 — Identify and prioritize research**](identify-and-prioritize-research/SKILL.md)
   develops and answers the questions needed for reliable understanding.
3. [**A3 — Analyze prior art**](analyze-prior-art/SKILL.md) examines relevant
   internal and external examples, patterns, products, and previous attempts.
4. [**A4 — Develop the Design Brief**](develop-design-brief/SKILL.md) brings the
   justified need, research, prior art, and shared language into one approved
   brief.

## Durable artifact

The inquiry document keeps the evidence and reasoning behind all four criteria.
Its sections use the exact criterion names above. The Design Brief is the
approved handoff to Develop Ideas, while the full inquiry document remains
available whenever later work needs its evidence.

## Movement

New work normally begins with A1 and proceeds through A4. Work can move to any
other criterion or phase when that move best answers the current question. For
example, A2 may move temporarily to B2 to create a focused prototype when
existing evidence cannot answer a research question. The prototype result
returns to the inquiry as research evidence and project-created prior art.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` before each move and keep shared terms current in
`GLOSSARY.md`.
