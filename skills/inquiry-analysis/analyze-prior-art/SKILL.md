# Analyze Prior Art

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to A3 before starting. Read the justified need, prioritized research, current inquiry document, glossary, and relevant project sources.

Prefer retrieval-led reasoning over training-led reasoning. Cite every example and the evidence used to describe it.

## Build the comparison set

Study how the project and the wider world have addressed the same or a nearby need. Unless the need is entirely project-specific, include at least as many external examples as internal examples.

Internal prior art may include existing code, established patterns, previous attempts, tests, docs, decisions, and relevant skills. External prior art may include comparable products, libraries, standards, papers, documented workflows, and implementations.

Reuse the inquiry-wide subagent permission for independent searches and analyses. Fan out distinct comparison areas in parallel, then reconcile overlapping or conflicting findings.

Include several credible examples when one familiar example could narrow the inquiry too early. Select examples for relevance and contrast, rather than quantity alone.

## Analyze each example

Record:

- what it is and its source;
- why it belongs in this inquiry;
- the need it addresses;
- how it works;
- evidence of its strengths and weaknesses;
- its limits;
- how this project differs;
- what later idea development can learn from it.

Use prior art to understand the landscape and extract lessons. `$start-develop-ideas` uses those lessons to develop and compare possible solution shapes.

When no existing example answers an important question, coordinate with `$identify-and-prioritize-research` to consider project-created prior art through a focused B2 prototype.

## Record the output

Write the **A3 — Analyze prior art** section of the inquiry document. Give each example a complete description and justification for inclusion.

A3 is complete when the human understands the examples, their relevance, their limits, and the lessons they provide.

Update `workflow-tracker.md` with the result, remaining questions, and recommended next move.
