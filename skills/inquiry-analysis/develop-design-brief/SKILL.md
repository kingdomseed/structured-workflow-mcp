# Develop the Design Brief

Update `workflow-tracker.md` to A4 before starting. Read the project inquiry template, current inquiry document, `GLOSSARY.md`, and the outputs of A1 through A3.

Prefer retrieval-led reasoning over training-led reasoning. Carry citations from the inquiry into the brief where they support material claims.

## Confirm readiness

Check that:

- A1 contains a clear and justified need;
- A2 records supported answers to the important research questions or names non-blocking unknowns;
- A3 describes and justifies relevant prior art and its lessons;
- shared terms have settled enough to discuss the work precisely.

Explain why the inquiry appears ready. Ask whether the human wants to develop the Design Brief together or continue investigating. Send a gap back to its owning criterion.

## Develop the brief

Use the project inquiry template. Develop the brief with the human from the settled inquiry evidence.

State:

- what the human wants to create, change, improve, prevent, preserve, or understand;
- who or what it is for;
- why it matters;
- the current situation and supporting evidence;
- the desired outcome;
- scope and boundaries;
- known constraints;
- what success looks like;
- the agreed language needed to discuss the work;
- remaining uncertainty that does not block idea development.

Describe the intended finished form. Preserve known requirements unless the human chooses a staged scope.

Write for a smart reader who has not seen the conversation. Use plain, exact words and active voice. Define needed technical terms. Use one term for each concept. Preserve the human's clear language. Make the smallest wording change that resolves a problem.

## Review and approve

Review the brief with the human against the evidence, glossary, project, and concrete scenarios. Check for vague claims, hidden blockers, missing people, unsupported assumptions, scope drift, and ambiguous terms.

Use Evaluate D1–D2 when an independent evidence-based review would help establish trust in the brief. Return each finding to the criterion that owns it.

A4 is complete when the human approves the Design Brief, its material claims are supported, its language is settled, and no known gap would force avoidable rework later.

Update the inquiry document and `workflow-tracker.md`. Recommend `$start-develop-ideas` when shaping the solution is the next useful work.
